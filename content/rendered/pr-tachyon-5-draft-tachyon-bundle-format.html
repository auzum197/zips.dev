#Terminology

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "MAY", and "RECOMMENDED" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 1 when, and only when, they appear in all capitals.

The term "network upgrade" is to be interpreted as described in ZIP 200.2 The terms "Testnet" and "Mainnet" are to be interpreted as described in § 3.12 ‘Mainnet and Testnet’. The character § is used when referring to sections of the Zcash Protocol Specification.3

txid, auth_digest, and the SIGHASH transaction hash are defined by ZIP 244 4; wtxid = txid || auth_digest, the 64-byte identifier used for transaction announcement and relay, by ZIP 239 5. Value commitments, spend authorization signatures, and binding signatures are existing constructions (§ 5.4.8.3 ‘Homomorphic Pedersen commitments (Sapling and Orchard)’, § 4.15 ‘Spend Authorization Signature (Sapling and Orchard)’, and § 4.14 ‘Balance and Binding Signature (Orchard)’, respectively, in their Orchard instantiations); this ZIP applies them to Tachyon as specified below rather than redefining them.

The following terms are defined by the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP and summarized here:

Tachygram
A Pallas base-field element representing a note commitment, a nullifier, or a padding value, as defined under Tachygrams. Its byte[32] wire representation is specified under Canonical encodings.
Anchor
A Poseidon hash-chain state referencing the Tachyon pool at a specific block. The chain advances at sub-block granularity, but consensus acknowledges only end-of-block states as anchors, as described in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Tachygram accumulator and Per-stamp checks.
Multiset commitment
As defined in Multiset commitments in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP. Its application to this format is described under Multiset commitments below.
Stamp
The proof-bearing protocol object defined under Stamp in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP. This ZIP defines two wire forms for a bundle's final section: a proof stamp carrying that object, or an empty stripped stamp.

The remaining terms are defined by this ZIP:

Bundle
The Tachyon section of a transaction: a body carrying actions, signatures, tachygrams, value balance, and fee data, followed by a state-dependent stamp.
Bundle state
The three-valued wire discriminator tachyonBundleState selecting no bundle, a proof stamp, or a stripped stamp.
Action
The triple (𝖼𝗏,𝗋𝗄,𝗌𝗂𝗀): a value commitment, a randomized verification key, and a signature over the transaction sighash. An action effects a spend or an output; both forms share this encoding, and consensus applies the same rules to each.
Action digest
The Poseidon digest binding an action's (𝖼𝗏,𝗋𝗄) descriptor to its unordered tachygram pair.
Descriptor digest
The BLAKE2b-256 digest of a sequence of action descriptors (see Action descriptor digests).
Proof stamp
A wire representation of a stamp, carrying a Ragu proof, an anchor, a tachygram multiset commitment, and the txids of the other transactions it covers. The proof attests that every covered action and fee satisfies the Tachyon protocol rules.
Stripped stamp
An empty wire form used when another transaction carries the covering proof.
Covering transaction
The proof-stamped transaction whose stamp covers its own bundle and the stripped bundles identified by its adjunct list.
Adjunct
A transaction whose Tachyon bundle is covered by another transaction's stamp. Its bundle is stripped when included in a block.

#Abstract

This ZIP is a living draft and does not prescribe whether the Tachyon bundle is deployed through ZIP 248's extensible transaction format6 or incorporated directly into a new transaction format. Its encoding and integration details may be revised as ZIP 248 and other related draft ZIPs evolve.

This ZIP specifies the consensus wire format of the Tachyon bundle: a three-state discriminator byte, a body carrying actions, signatures, tachygrams, value balance, and fee data, and either a proof stamp or an empty stripped stamp. It defines the canonical field encodings and sequence orders, the action and fee digests, and the use of the shielded protocol's multiset commitments in bundle verification. It further defines the bundle's transaction-digest inputs, the consensus rules scoped to a single bundle, and the block-scoped rules that validate a block's bundles together. It defines the transaction representation of the Tachyon pool, just as ZIP 225 7 defines the v5 transaction fields for the Orchard shielded protocol.

#Motivation

To improve scalability, the Tachyon shielded protocol introduces support for proof aggregation: the stamps of many transactions merge into one covering stamp, and covered transactions appear in a block with their stamp stripped. The transaction format must therefore allow a stamp to be removed without changing the transaction's identity and without invalidating any signature. The body separates effecting data from signing and proving data. Its contents, including tachygrams and fees, remain with their originating transaction when the stamp is stripped.

A single format serves every transaction role: a proof stamp covers its own bundle and lists the txids of any other covered transactions. A stripped bundle retains the data needed for signature, balance, and proof verification; block validation resolves coverage from the proof stamps' adjunct lists.

#Requirements

  • A transaction's txid contribution is invariant across stamping, merging, stripping, re-stamping, and fee adjustments.
  • Proof-stamped and stripped forms carry identical bodies; only the state byte and stamp differ.
  • Every field a validator needs for signature and balance verification is present in both states.
  • Encodings are canonical: each parsed bundle has exactly one serialization.
  • The proof field is length-prefixed, with its size bounded before allocation.
  • Bundles with no actions are representable, under both stamp forms.

#Non-requirements

This ZIP does not independently define:

  • the multiset commitment construction, which is specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP;
  • the proof statement, which is specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Stamp, Proof tree, and Dynamic fees;
  • the tachygram accumulator, anchor semantics and validity rules, and cross-block duplicate-tachygram checks within the retained epoch window, which are specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Tachygram accumulator, Epochs, and Per-stamp checks;
  • the complete transaction digest trees or sighash algorithm. These use the ZIP 244 framework 4, with Tachyon-specific integration still to be specified as noted under Transaction digest contributions;
  • P2P aggregation messages, covered-transaction discovery, or mempool and relay policy;
  • the position of the bundle section within the transaction encoding, which is specified by the transaction format of the activating network upgrade.

#Specification

The specification proceeds from the wire layout of the bundle body to the rules over its fields, then to the digest constructions and multiset commitment inputs those rules use, then to the two stamp forms. It closes with the canonical encodings of every field, the bundle's transaction-digest inputs, a consolidated summary of the bundle-scoped consensus rules, and the block-scoped rules that validate a block's bundles together.

The Tachyon bundle is a contiguous section of the transaction encoding, added by a Tachyon network upgrade. The first byte of the section, tachyonBundleState, selects the bundle state:

#Bundle body

When tachyonBundleState is not 0x00, the body follows the discriminator byte, laid out as in the wire layout above.

valueBalanceTachyon is a two's-complement signed 64-bit integer in little-endian byte order. vActionsTachyon is a sequence of nActionsTachyon action descriptors, each the 32-byte encoding of 𝖼𝗏 followed by the 32-byte encoding of 𝗋𝗄. vActionSigsTachyon is a sequence of nActionsTachyon 64-byte signatures; the i-th signature authorizes the i-th descriptor. vActionTachygramsTachyon contains one pair of tachygrams per action, in the same order. All three sequences share the single count nActionsTachyon, so a count mismatch between descriptors, signatures, and pairs is unrepresentable. The descriptor sequence's order is the transaction author's choice. The semantics of spends and outputs are described in the shielded-protocol ZIP's Proof tree section.

cvFeeMaxTachyon is the maximum-fee note's value commitment, included in the effecting data. The actual fee, vFeeTachyon, and fee change note commitment, cmChangeTachyon, are proving data in the body. The fee mechanism is specified under Dynamic fees in the shielded-protocol ZIP.

The body's vActionTachygramsTachyon and cmChangeTachyon contain exactly 2⋅𝗇𝖠𝖼𝗍𝗂𝗈𝗇𝗌𝖳𝖺𝖼𝗁𝗒𝗈𝗇+1 tachygrams. A parser MUST reject any duplicate among these values, in either bundle state. Their roles are specified under Tachygrams.

vMemoTachyon is an opaque recipient-directed payload of nMemoTachyon bytes; nMemoTachyon of 0 encodes an absent payload. The memo contributes to txid and the sighash (Transaction digest contributions) rather than to auth_digest, so it is identical across bundle states.

#Value balance and the binding signature

valueBalanceTachyon equals spends minus ordinary outputs minus the maximum fee. Transaction balance and pool accounting use valueBalanceTachyon + vFeeTachyon: a positive sum releases value from the Tachyon pool; a negative sum transfers value into it.

Tachyon reuses Orchard's value commitments8 and binding-key derivation and binding-signature construction,9 applied to the bundle's action value commitments and valueBalanceTachyon, with cvFeeMaxTachyon included as an additional output commitment. bindingSigTachyon MUST verify over the transaction sighash under the resulting binding validating key 𝖻𝗏𝗄, which is derived rather than serialized. The actual fee does not enter this key calculation.

#Action signatures

Each action signature in vActionSigsTachyon MUST be a valid spend authorization signature (§ 4.15 ‘Spend Authorization Signature (Sapling and Orchard)’; RedPallas with the SpendAuth basepoint of § 5.4.7.1, for spends and outputs alike) over the transaction sighash under the corresponding action's 𝗋𝗄. All of a bundle's signatures sign the same transaction-level sighash. The Tachyon-specific inputs and pending integration with the ZIP 244 framework 4 are described under Transaction digest contributions.

#Action digests

Each action digest binds its (𝖼𝗏,𝗋𝗄) descriptor to its unordered tachygram pair (t0,t1) from vActionTachygramsTachyon. Let (𝖼𝗏x,𝖼𝗏y) and (𝗋𝗄x,𝗋𝗄y) be the affine Pallas coordinates of the action's decompressed 𝖼𝗏 and 𝗋𝗄. Then

𝖼𝗏x, 𝖼𝗏y, 𝗋𝗄x, 𝗋𝗄y, t0+t1, t0t1).

The tagged Poseidon instance initializes the rate to zero and the capacity to the Pallas base-field element whose integer value is the little-endian interpretation of the 16-byte ASCII string Tachyon-ActionDg. It absorbs only the six displayed field elements. The width, rounds, round constants, and mode are fixed by the Ragu proof system.10

If an action's 𝖼𝗏 or 𝗋𝗄 is the identity point, it has no affine coordinates, the digest is undefined, and the transaction is invalid.

#Action descriptor digests

An action's descriptor is its 64-byte encoding in vActionsTachyon: the 32-byte encoding of 𝖼𝗏 followed by the 32-byte encoding of 𝗋𝗄. The descriptor digest of a sequence of actions is the BLAKE2b-256 hash, with personalization Tachyon-Actions, of the concatenation of their descriptors:

"Tachyon-Actions", 𝖼𝗏1‖𝗋𝗄1‖⋯‖𝖼𝗏n‖𝗋𝗄n)

The digest of the empty sequence is the hash of the empty string under the same personalization.

hActionsTachyon, an input to the effecting digest contribution, is computed over the bundle's own actions in their vActionsTachyon wire order. It is not carried on the wire.

#Fee descriptors and digests

Each covered bundle contributes one fee descriptor, the pair (cvFeeMaxTachyon, vFeeTachyon). Its 40-byte encoding is the commitment's 32-byte encoding followed by the fee's 8-byte little-endian encoding. The descriptor is assembled from body fields, not serialized separately.

For affine Pallas coordinates (𝖼𝗏max⁡,x,𝖼𝗏max⁡,y) of the maximum-fee commitment, its fee digest also binds the bundle's fee change:

𝖼𝗏max⁡,x, 𝖼𝗏max⁡,y, 𝖿𝖾𝖾, 𝖼𝗆change).

Here 𝖿𝖾𝖾 is vFeeTachyon encoded as a Pallas base-field element and 𝖼𝗆change is cmChangeTachyon. The tagged Poseidon instance is specified under Action digests, with the capacity initialized from Tachyon-FeeDg padded with three zero bytes to 16 bytes. cvFeeMaxTachyon MUST NOT be the identity point, which has no affine coordinates.11

Descriptor distinctness is checked over the 40-byte pair, excluding cmChangeTachyon; the fee digest binds all three values.

#Multiset commitments

Multiset commitments MUST use the construction specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP.

Each proof stamp binds two commitments: cStampActionsTachyon, over the action and fee digests it covers, and cTachygrams, over the union of the covered bodies' tachygrams. Validators reconstruct cStampActionsTachyon from the covered action descriptors and their pairs, and fee descriptors and their change commitments; it is not serialized. The stamp carries cTachygrams, which validators MUST check against the commitment reconstructed from those same pairs and change commitments, as specified under Block validity.

#Proof stamp

When tachyonBundleState is 0x01, the proof stamp follows the body, laid out as in the wire layout above.

vAdjunctsTachyon lists the txid of every other transaction whose bundle the stamp covers. The carrying transaction MUST NOT appear in its own list. A stamp covering only its own bundle has an empty list. The list is strictly increasing by bytewise comparison of the 32-byte txids, excluding duplicates. Block validity specifies how these references are resolved.

anchorTachyon references the pool state the proof is valid against; its semantics and the anchor-membership rule are specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Tachygram accumulator and Per-stamp checks.

cTachygrams is the multiset commitment (Multiset commitments) over the union of vActionTachygramsTachyon and cmChangeTachyon from the stamp's own bundle and every listed adjunct. Validators MUST reconstruct this union and confirm its commitment against the carried point.

A stamp covering N actions across B bundles commits to 2N+B tachygrams, which MUST be distinct and number at most 8191. Both counts include the stamp's own bundle and every transaction it covers. A parser MUST reject nAdjunctsTachyon greater than 8190 before reading the list, and MUST reject a proof-stamped bundle if 2⋅𝗇𝖠𝖼𝗍𝗂𝗈𝗇𝗌𝖳𝖺𝖼𝗁𝗒𝗈𝗇+𝗇𝖠𝖽𝗃𝗎𝗇𝖼𝗍𝗌𝖳𝖺𝖼𝗁𝗒𝗈𝗇+1>8191. These bounds follow from the maximum degree of the tachygram multiset polynomial.12

Block-level distinctness is specified under Block validity; cross-block distinctness within the retained epoch window is specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Epochs and Per-stamp checks.

proofTachyon is the Ragu proof. The statement it attests to, and the base rule that a stamp proof MUST verify against the Tachyon statement, are specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Stamp, Proof tree, and Per-stamp checks; how that rule is applied to a block's stamps is specified in Block validity.

#Stripped stamp

When tachyonBundleState is 0x02, no fields follow the body. Stripping preserves the entire body, including its tachygram pairs, actual fee, and fee change note commitment. The covering transaction lists this transaction's txid; the stripped bundle carries no reference to its covering transaction. Block validity requires exactly one covering proof stamp.

#Canonical encodings

  • Every compactSize field MUST use the minimal encoding for its value (§ 7.1 ‘Transaction Encoding and Consensus’) and MUST NOT encode a value exceeding 0x02000000. A parser MUST reject any other encoding.
  • cv, rk, and cvFeeMaxTachyon are 32-byte compressed encodings of Pallas points. A parser MUST reject an encoding that does not decode to a point. rk MUST decode as a RedPallas validating key (§ 5.4.7 ‘RedDSA, RedJubjub, and RedPallas’). The identity point is excluded by the rules in Action digests and Fee descriptors and digests.
  • vFeeTachyon is an unsigned 64-bit little-endian integer in zatoshis and MUST be at most MAX_MONEY.
  • anchorTachyon, cmChangeTachyon, and each action tachygram are canonical little-endian encodings of Pallas base field elements; a parser MUST reject an encoding whose value is not less than the field modulus.
  • vActionsTachyon carries no ordering requirement: the descriptors may appear in any sequence the transaction author chooses. The signatures and tachygram pairs follow their descriptors' positions.
  • Either order within a tachygram pair is valid. Provers SHOULD place the numerically smaller field element first to avoid disclosing each member's role. The authorization digest hashes pairs in their wire order.
  • vAdjunctsTachyon MUST be strictly increasing by bytewise comparison of its 32-byte txids; a parser MUST reject repeated or out-of-order entries.
  • cTachygrams is a 32-byte compressed Vesta point; a parser MUST reject an encoding that does not decode to a nonidentity point. Whether it commits to the covered bodies' tachygrams is a block-validity property (Block validity).
  • vMemoTachyon is an opaque byte string at parse time; this ZIP imposes no structure on its contents.
  • Signatures (vActionSigsTachyon, bindingSigTachyon) are opaque 64-byte strings at parse time; their validity is a verification-time property.
  • proofTachyon MUST decode as a Ragu proof of exactly sizeProofTachyon bytes, including its envelope. A parser MUST reject a length exceeding the proof type's Ragu transport bound before allocating the frame, and MUST reject an invalid envelope, truncated proof, or trailing bytes within the frame. Block inclusion requires the Compressed proof form. Uncompressed proofs use the same layout for merging and lifting before inclusion.12

#Bundle validity

The rules owned by this ZIP, applying to a single transaction's bundle:

  1. tachyonBundleState MUST be 0x00, 0x01, or 0x02.
  2. Every compactSize MUST be minimally encoded and MUST NOT exceed 0x02000000.
  3. Every point and field-element encoding MUST be canonical, and every sequence in canonical order, as specified in Canonical encodings.
  4. An action's 𝖼𝗏 and 𝗋𝗄, and cvFeeMaxTachyon, MUST NOT be the identity point.
  5. Every action signature MUST verify over the transaction sighash under its action's 𝗋𝗄.
  6. bindingSigTachyon MUST verify over the transaction sighash under the derived 𝖻𝗏𝗄.
  7. valueBalanceTachyon MUST be in the range −MAX_MONEY to MAX_MONEY inclusive.
  8. A bundle with no actions MUST have valueBalanceTachyon and vFeeTachyon equal to 0.
  9. A proof stamp's proofTachyon MUST satisfy the length, envelope, and proof-form rules under Canonical encodings.
  10. The tachygrams in a bundle's action pairs and fee change commitment MUST be distinct.
  11. A proof stamp's adjunct list MUST be strictly increasing, MUST NOT include its own transaction's txid, and MUST contain at most 8190 entries.
  12. vFeeTachyon MUST be in the range 0 to MAX_MONEY inclusive.
  13. nActionsTachyon MUST be at most 4095. A proof-stamped bundle MUST also satisfy 2⋅𝗇𝖠𝖼𝗍𝗂𝗈𝗇𝗌𝖳𝖺𝖼𝗁𝗒𝗈𝗇+𝗇𝖠𝖽𝗃𝗎𝗇𝖼𝗍𝗌𝖳𝖺𝖼𝗁𝗒𝗈𝗇+1≤8191.

Rules outside the scope of this ZIP are enumerated in Non-requirements.

#Block validity

The rules owned by this ZIP that constrain a block's Tachyon bundles together. A validator enforces them fail-fast, in this order:

  1. Tachygram uniqueness. All tachygrams in a block MUST be distinct. Collect vActionTachygramsTachyon and cmChangeTachyon from every bundle body, whether proof-stamped or stripped, and reject any duplicate. Reuse across blocks within the retained epoch window is governed by the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP's Per-stamp checks.
  2. Proof coverage. Every txid in a proof stamp's vAdjunctsTachyon MUST identify exactly one transaction in the same block, whose Tachyon bundle MUST be stripped. Every stripped bundle MUST be listed by exactly one proof stamp, including bundles with no actions. A validator MUST check for unlisted stripped bundles across the entire block; traversing adjunct lists alone is insufficient.14
  3. Covered descriptors and tachygram bound. For each proof stamp, collect its own bundle and the stripped bundles listed by its vAdjunctsTachyon. Their action descriptors MUST be mutually distinct, as MUST their fee descriptors. For N covered actions and B covered bundles, 2N+B MUST NOT exceed 8191. Reject repeated descriptors or an exceeded bound before curve arithmetic.
  4. Tachygram commitment. Reconstruct the multiset commitment over the action pairs and fee change commitments from those covered bodies (Multiset commitments). It MUST equal the stamp's carried cTachygrams.
  5. Proof verification. Every proof stamp MUST verify against its anchor, the cTachygrams confirmed by rule 4, and cStampActionsTachyon reconstructed from the covered action and fee digests (Action digests, Fee descriptors and digests, Multiset commitments). The base requirement that a proof verifies the Tachyon statement is specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP's Per-stamp checks; this rule applies it per stamp within a block.

#Rationale

This subsection is non-normative.

#The stamp is excluded from the txid contribution

The effecting contribution excludes the stamp, so stamping, merging, stripping, re-stamping, and fee adjustments preserve a transaction's logical identity. This allows proof aggregation without changing the transaction its author signed: a stripped transaction retains the same body and signatures.

#Zero-action balance

The v5 analogue omits valueBalanceOrchard when the action count is zero and defines its value as zero in that case. Tachyon gates field presence on the bundle state, not the action count: the non-tachyon state omits the field, while a present bundle always encodes valueBalanceTachyon. A present bundle with no actions therefore still carries the field, so the zero balance is stated as an explicit rule. Together with the binding signature, this requires a zero maximum fee; the dynamic fee mechanism also requires a zero actual fee. Its fee descriptor and fee change note commitment remain part of the covering proof.

#Identity points are invalid

The action and fee digests hash affine coordinates, which the identity point lacks. Excluding it also rejects a degenerate verification key and a degenerate value commitment.

#Adjunct txids are sorted

Sorting vAdjunctsTachyon gives each adjunct set one wire encoding and excludes repeated entries. The list identifies the covered bundles directly; validators reconstruct the proof inputs from those bodies.

#Covered actions are required to be distinct

Two identical descriptors carry an identical 𝗋𝗄, so the same signature would authorize both if they sign the same transaction sighash. Multiset commitments preserve multiplicity and do not establish distinctness. Block validity rule 3 therefore rejects repeated action or fee descriptors before commitment reconstruction and proof verification.

#Tachygram pairs are unordered

The action digest binds a pair through its sum and product, so swapping its two members preserves the proof statement. Either wire order is accepted, while the authorization digest records the order actually transmitted. A prover sorts each pair numerically to avoid revealing which member is a commitment, nullifier, or padding value.

#Deterministic multiset commitments

The committed multisets are public data, so a hiding commitment is unnecessary; determinism is what lets any party recompute a commitment from the data it covers.

#Length-prefixed proof

A length prefix frames the Ragu proof and its envelope. Bounding that length before allocation limits untrusted input; exact decoding rejects extra bytes within the frame.

#Adjuncts are referenced by txid

An aggregate lists its adjuncts, so lifting or rerandomizing the aggregate does not require rewriting them. A txid identifies effecting data; the covering proof additionally binds each adjunct's actual fee, fee change, and unordered action pairs. A body with different values fails verification.

#Security Implications

This subsection is non-normative.

#Canonical encodings and ordering

The canonical-encoding rules restrict field encodings and adjunct-list order; they do not imply that a transaction has only one valid proof, set of signatures, or ordering of each tachygram pair. Swapping a pair changes auth_digest and wtxid without changing its proof statement. Reordering distinct action descriptors changes hActionsTachyon, and therefore the sighash every signature covers, so existing signatures do not authorize the reordered transaction. Leaving action order to the author's choice is the same property Sapling and Orchard rely on for their own unordered spend, output, and action arrays. Authorization-form changes (re-stamping, stripping) produce distinct bundles by design and are reflected in auth_digest and wtxid (ZIP 239 5).

#Duplicate actions are gated after parsing, not by the parser

The parser enforces no distinctness over vActionsTachyon, so a bundle carrying a repeated action descriptor parses. Each descriptor's 𝖼𝗏 enters the binding sum, so action multiplicity is load-bearing and the parser cannot deduplicate without changing the asserted balance; the action digests enter the stamp's cStampActionsTachyon alongside its covered fee digests.

Block validity rule 3 explicitly rejects repeated action or fee descriptors in a proof stamp's combined coverage before commitment reconstruction. This includes duplicates within one bundle and across bundles covered by the same stamp. The multiset commitment preserves repeated roots; it does not itself prohibit them. A parser must therefore preserve multiplicity rather than silently deduplicate the actions.

Repeated tachygrams in a body's action pairs or fee change commitment violate the explicit distinctness rule, which the parser enforces directly (Bundle validity rule 10).

#Complete proof coverage

A stripped bundle retains all its actions and balance data but carries no proof. An unlisted stripped bundle would therefore leave those values unproven. Block validation must check all stripped bundles for coverage, not only those reached through proof stamps' adjunct lists. A listed txid does not itself establish proof coverage: the reconstructed commitments and proof must also verify.

#Balance consistency is enforced by the binding property

A valid binding signature establishes that valueBalanceTachyon equals the net value committed by the actions' 𝖼𝗏 together with cvFeeMaxTachyon, by the same binding argument as Orchard (§ 4.14).

#Signatures survive stripping

All signatures cover the transaction sighash, which incorporates only effecting data. A miner stripping a proof stamp changes no signed data, so aggregation does not invalidate signatures. A signature authorizes only the sighash it signs; using an action in a transaction with different effecting data requires a new signature over that transaction's sighash.

Parse validity is not spend validity. A bundle that parses and whose signatures verify is not thereby valid to spend; the Block validity rules and the shielded protocol's Consensus rules also apply. Implementers and auditors should not assume the rules in this ZIP alone establish those properties.

#Privacy Implications

This subsection is non-normative.

#Public data

𝖼𝗏 is a hiding commitment to the action's value; cvFeeMaxTachyon hides the maximum fee. The privacy of the underlying notes and keys depends on the shielded protocol; see Keys and addresses and Tachygrams. Payload confidentiality and note discovery depend on the payment protocol, as discussed under Privacy and Security Implications. The action count, value balance, actual fee, each action's tachygram pair, and the fee change note commitment are public. The pair is associated with its action, but its members are untyped. The separate fee change field identifies that tachygram as a note commitment.

Comparing versions of a transaction re-proved for different epochs reveals which action pairs change (spends) and which stay fixed (outputs). A fee adjustment alone leaves the action pairs unchanged.15 A delegate that knows a note's nullifier window can recognize its spending action from the published pair.16

Aggregation does not hide in-block coverage: proof stamps publicly list their adjuncts' txids, including for bundles with no actions (see Block validity).
