#Terminology

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", and "MAY" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 1 when, and only when, they appear in all capitals.

The term "network upgrade" is to be interpreted as described in ZIP 200.2 The terms "Testnet" and "Mainnet" are to be interpreted as described in § 3.12 ‘Mainnet and Testnet’. The character § is used when referring to sections of the Zcash Protocol Specification.3

txid, auth_digest, and the SIGHASH transaction hash are defined by ZIP 244 4; wtxid = txid || auth_digest, the 64-byte identifier used for transaction announcement and relay, by ZIP 239 5. Value commitments, spend authorization signatures, and binding signatures are existing constructions (§ 5.4.8.3 ‘Homomorphic Pedersen commitments (Sapling and Orchard)’, § 4.15 ‘Spend Authorization Signature (Sapling and Orchard)’, and § 4.14 ‘Balance and Binding Signature (Orchard)’, respectively, in their Orchard instantiations); this ZIP applies them to Tachyon as specified below rather than redefining them.

The following terms are defined by the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP and summarized here:

Tachygram
A Pallas base-field element representing a note commitment, a nullifier, or a padding value, as defined under Tachygrams. Its byte[32] wire representation is specified under Canonical encodings.
Anchor
A Poseidon hash-chain state referencing the Tachyon pool at a specific block. The chain advances at sub-block granularity, but consensus acknowledges only end-of-block states as anchors, as described in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Tachygram accumulator and Per-stamp checks.
Multiset commitment
As defined in Multiset commitments in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP. Its application to this format is described under Multiset commitments below.
Stamp
The proof-bearing protocol object defined under Stamp in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP. This ZIP defines two wire forms for a bundle's final section: a proof stamp carrying that object, or a stripped stamp retaining the bundle's tachygrams and fee.

The remaining terms are defined by this ZIP:

Bundle
The Tachyon section of a transaction: actions, a value balance, a maximum-fee commitment, signatures, an opaque payload, and a state-dependent stamp trailer.
Bundle state
The three-valued wire discriminator tachyonBundleState selecting no bundle, a proof stamp, or a stripped stamp.
Action
The triple (𝖼𝗏,𝗋𝗄,𝗌𝗂𝗀): a value commitment, a randomized verification key, and a signature over the transaction sighash. An action effects a spend or an output; both forms share this encoding, and consensus applies the same rules to each.
Action digest
The Poseidon digest binding an action's (𝖼𝗏,𝗋𝗄) descriptor to its unordered tachygram pair (see Action digests).
Descriptor digest
The BLAKE2b-256 digest of a sequence of action descriptors (see Action descriptor digests).
Proof stamp
A trailer carrying the bundle's own tachygrams and fee, an anchor, a commitment to the covered tachygram union, a list of other covered transactions' txids, and a Ragu proof covering the actions and fee descriptors of those bundles.
Stripped stamp
A trailer retaining only the carrying bundle's tachygrams and fee.
Adjunct
A transaction whose Tachyon bundle has a stripped stamp.
Covering transaction
A proof-stamped transaction whose vAdjunctsTachyon lists an adjunct's txid. Its proof also covers its own bundle, which is not included in that list.
Fee descriptor
The tuple of a bundle's maximum-fee value commitment cvFeeMaxTachyon, applied fee vFeeTachyon, and fee change note commitment, the last entry of its vTachygrams. See Fees.

#Abstract

This ZIP is a living draft and does not prescribe whether the Tachyon bundle is deployed through ZIP 248's extensible transaction format6 or incorporated directly into a new transaction format. Its encoding and integration details may be revised as ZIP 248 and other related draft ZIPs evolve.

This revision follows the transaction-local tachygrams and aggregate-to-adjunct txid references proposed in Tachyon issue #226 and implemented in draft PR #280, at revision e6bcdf1.7 8

This ZIP specifies the consensus wire format of the Tachyon bundle: a three-state discriminator byte, a common body, and a proof or stripped stamp trailer. Both trailers retain the bundle's own tachygrams and fee. A proof stamp additionally carries the proof, anchor, tachygram-union commitment, and covered-transaction list. It defines the canonical field encodings and sequence orders, the action and descriptor digests, and the use of the shielded protocol's multiset commitments in bundle verification. It further defines the bundle's transaction-digest inputs, the consensus rules scoped to a single bundle, and the block-scoped rules that validate a block's bundles together. It defines the transaction representation of the Tachyon pool, just as ZIP 225 9 defines the v5 transaction fields for the Orchard shielded protocol.

#Motivation

To improve scalability, the Tachyon shielded protocol supports proof aggregation. A covering transaction carries a merged proof and lists the txids of the other transactions it covers. Those transactions retain their actions, signatures, tachygrams, and fees when their proofs are stripped for block inclusion.

Keeping tachygrams with their originating transactions preserves their association for note retrieval. Binding each pair into its action digest makes that association verifiable. The explicit txid list identifies the transactions needed to validate an aggregate.7

The format separates effecting data from authorization data so that aggregation and stripping preserve txid and existing signatures. A stripped bundle's bytes depend on that bundle alone, allowing its covering proof to change without rewriting a reference in the adjunct.

#Requirements

  • A transaction's txid contribution is invariant across stamping, merging, stripping, and re-stamping.
  • Proof-stamped and stripped forms carry identical effecting data and signatures.
  • Stripping preserves the bundle's own tachygrams and applied fee.
  • Every field needed for signature and balance verification is present in both states.
  • Aggregate coverage is identified by an explicit list of covered transaction txids.
  • Parsers bound action counts, adjunct counts, and proof lengths before allocation.
  • Bundles with no actions are representable under both stamp forms.

#Non-requirements

This ZIP does not independently define:

  • the multiset commitment construction, which is specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP;
  • the proof statement, which is specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Stamp and Proof tree;
  • the tachygram accumulator, anchor semantics and validity rules, and cross-block duplicate-tachygram checks within the retained epoch window, which are specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP under Tachygram accumulator, Epochs, and Per-stamp checks;
  • the complete transaction digest trees or sighash algorithm. These use the ZIP 244 framework 4, with integration still to be specified as noted under Transaction digest contributions;
  • P2P aggregation messages, covered-transaction discovery, or mempool and relay policy;
  • the position of the bundle section within the transaction encoding, which is specified by the transaction format of the activating network upgrade.

#Specification

The specification proceeds from the wire layout of the bundle body to the rules over its fields, then to the digest constructions and multiset commitment inputs those rules use, then to the two stamp forms. It closes with the canonical encodings of every field, the bundle's transaction-digest inputs, a consolidated summary of the bundle-scoped consensus rules, and the block-scoped rules that validate a block's bundles together.

#Bundle body

When tachyonBundleState is not 0x00, the body follows the discriminator byte, laid out as in the wire layout above.

valueBalanceTachyon is a two's-complement signed 64-bit integer in little-endian byte order. vActionsTachyon is a sequence of nActionsTachyon action descriptors, each the 32-byte encoding of 𝖼𝗏 followed by the 32-byte encoding of 𝗋𝗄. vActionSigsTachyon is a sequence of nActionsTachyon 64-byte signatures; the i-th signature authorizes the i-th descriptor. Both sequences share the single count nActionsTachyon, so a count mismatch between descriptors and signatures is unrepresentable. The descriptor sequence's order is the transaction author's choice. The semantics of spends and outputs are described in the shielded-protocol ZIP's Proof tree section.

vMemoTachyon is an opaque recipient-directed payload of nMemoTachyon bytes; nMemoTachyon of 0 encodes an absent payload. The memo contributes to txid and the sighash (Transaction digest contributions) rather than to auth_digest, so it is identical across bundle states.

#Value balance and the binding signature

valueBalanceTachyon counts the maximum-fee note as an output: total value spent minus ordinary outputs minus the maximum fee. The applied fee is selected later, and the remaining value returns as a fee change note, as specified under Fees. The bundle's net pool outflow is valueBalanceTachyon + vFeeTachyon.11

Tachyon reuses Orchard's value commitments12 and binding signature construction.13 The derived binding validating key is

−[𝗏𝖺𝗅𝗎𝖾𝖡𝖺𝗅𝖺𝗇𝖼𝖾𝖳𝖺𝖼𝗁𝗒𝗈𝗇]𝒱,

where 𝖼𝗏𝖿𝖾𝖾𝖬𝖺𝗑 is cvFeeMaxTachyon and 𝒱 is Orchard's value generator. bindingSigTachyon MUST verify over the transaction sighash under this key. The applied fee is excluded from the binding-key derivation.

#Action signatures

Each action signature in vActionSigsTachyon MUST be a valid spend authorization signature (§ 4.15 ‘Spend Authorization Signature (Sapling and Orchard)’; RedPallas with the SpendAuth basepoint of § 5.4.7.1, for spends and outputs alike) over the transaction sighash under the corresponding action's 𝗋𝗄. All of a bundle's signatures sign the same transaction-level sighash. The Tachyon-specific inputs and pending integration with the ZIP 244 framework 4 are described under Transaction digest contributions.

#Action digests

Let (𝖼𝗏x,𝖼𝗏y) and (𝗋𝗄x,𝗋𝗄y) be the affine Pallas coordinates of an action's value commitment and randomized key. Let t0,t1 be its two Pallas base-field tachygrams, taken from the corresponding pair in its bundle's vTachygrams. Its action digest is the Pallas base-field element

𝖼𝗏x,𝖼𝗏y,𝗋𝗄x,𝗋𝗄y,t0+t1,t0t1).

The sum and product bind the pair without distinguishing its order. The proof MUST bind the same pair to the action and to the covered tachygram multiset. The tagged Poseidon sponge initializes its rate to zero and its capacity to the little-endian integer represented by the domain's ASCII bytes, zero-padded to 16 bytes, and absorbs the listed data elements.14 The shielded protocol specifies the proof constraints.15

If cv or rk is the identity point, the digest is undefined and the transaction is invalid.

#Fee digests

For each covered bundle, let 𝖼𝗏𝖿𝖾𝖾𝖬𝖺𝗑 be its decompressed cvFeeMaxTachyon, f its integer vFeeTachyon embedded in the Pallas base field, and 𝖼𝗆𝖼𝗁𝗀 the last entry of its vTachygrams. Its fee digest is

x(𝖼𝗏𝖿𝖾𝖾𝖬𝖺𝗑),y(𝖼𝗏𝖿𝖾𝖾𝖬𝖺𝗑),f,𝖼𝗆𝖼𝗁𝗀).

This digest binds the fee change commitment to its bundle's fee descriptor. The proof constraints are specified under Fees.11

#Action descriptor digests

An action's descriptor is its 64-byte encoding in vActionsTachyon: the 32-byte encoding of 𝖼𝗏 followed by the 32-byte encoding of 𝗋𝗄. The descriptor digest of a sequence of actions is the BLAKE2b-256 hash, with personalization Tachyon-Actions, of the concatenation of their descriptors:

"Tachyon-Actions", 𝖼𝗏1‖𝗋𝗄1‖⋯‖𝖼𝗏n‖𝗋𝗄n)

The digest of the empty sequence is the hash of the empty string under the same personalization.

hActionsTachyon uses this construction over the bundle's own descriptors in their vActionsTachyon wire order. It contributes to the effecting digest and is not serialized. Tachygram pairs are excluded from this descriptor hash; the proof binds them through the separate Action digests.

#Multiset commitments

Multiset commitments MUST use the construction specified in the Tachyon Shielded Protocol ZIP.

Validators reconstruct cStampActionsTachyon from every covered action digest and one fee digest per covered bundle. This commitment is not serialized. They also reconstruct cTachygrams from the union of those bundles' vTachygrams and MUST check it against the carried commitment. The proof binds both reconstructed multisets, as specified under Block validity.

#Proof stamp

When tachyonBundleState is 0x01, the proof stamp follows the body.

vTachygrams contains only the carrying bundle's two tachygrams per action, in descriptor order, followed by its fee change note commitment. There is no separate tachygram count. These entries remain in the same transaction through aggregation and stripping.

vAdjunctsTachyon lists the 32-byte txid of every other transaction covered by the proof. The carrying transaction is implicitly covered and MUST NOT appear in its own list. A proof covering only its carrying bundle has an empty list. Each listed transaction MUST be stripped and present in the same block. Every stripped bundle MUST be listed exactly once across the block's proof stamps, including bundles with no actions (Block validity).

anchorTachyon references the pool state against which the proof is valid; anchor and epoch rules are specified in the shielded-protocol ZIP's Per-stamp checks. cTachygrams commits to the union of the carrying bundle's tachygrams and those of the listed bundles. It MUST match the reconstructed commitment.

For N covered actions in B covered bundles, the union has 2N+B members, all distinct, and MUST contain at most 8191 members. This bound follows from the production polynomial capacity of 8192 coefficients.10

proofTachyon is a Ragu-encoded proof of the shielded protocol's Proof tree statements. Block validation MUST use the compressed consensus proof form. sizeProofTachyon gives its encoded byte length; a parser MUST reject lengths above the Ragu transport bound before allocating the proof buffer, and MUST reject invalid envelopes or lengths.10

The adjunct list is authorization data included in auth_digest. It is not signed or itself an input to the stamp proof. Validators MUST resolve the list and verify coverage against the actual bundles in the block. The proof binds their action pairs and fee descriptors, including the fee change commitments. 16

#Stripped stamp

When tachyonBundleState is 0x02, the stripped trailer follows the body. It preserves the bundle's vTachygrams and vFeeTachyon. It contains no proof, anchor, tachygram-union commitment, or reference to a covering transaction.

Stripping removes the proof stamp's additional fields without changing the body, tachygrams, or fee. The covering proof stamp identifies this transaction by its txid. See Block validity for coverage requirements.

#Canonical encodings

  • Every compactSize field MUST use its minimal encoding and MUST NOT exceed 0x02000000; narrower bounds below also apply.
  • nActionsTachyon MUST be at most 4095. A proof-stamp parser MUST reject 2 * nActionsTachyon + nAdjunctsTachyon + 2 > 8192 before reading the adjunct list. Every covered bundle contributes at least its fee change tachygram.
  • cv, rk, and cvFeeMaxTachyon are 32-byte compressed Pallas points. Their encodings MUST be canonical; rk MUST decode as a RedPallas validating key. All three points MUST be non-identity.
  • anchorTachyon and each tachygram are canonical little-endian encodings of Pallas base-field elements; encodings at least the field modulus MUST be rejected.
  • vActionsTachyon is in the author's chosen order. Signatures and tachygram pairs follow the corresponding descriptor positions.
  • Each tachygram pair MAY appear in either order. The reference prover emits the smaller member first, but parsing MUST preserve the received order. Reordering a pair changes auth_digest without changing its action digest. There is no global sort of vTachygrams.10
  • vAdjunctsTachyon MUST be strictly ascending by bytewise comparison of txids. A parser MUST reject repeats or a different order.
  • cTachygrams MUST be a canonical compressed non-identity Vesta point. Whether it commits to the covered bundles' tachygrams is checked during block validation.
  • vFeeTachyon is an unsigned 64-bit little-endian integer and MUST NOT exceed MAX_MONEY. valueBalanceTachyon is a signed 64-bit little-endian integer in the inclusive range -MAX_MONEY to MAX_MONEY.
  • vMemoTachyon is opaque. Signatures are 64-byte strings whose validity is checked during signature verification.
  • proofTachyon MUST satisfy the length and decoding rules in Proof stamp.

#Bundle validity

These checks apply to every bundle, including stripped bundles:

  1. The state byte, encodings, counts, ranges, and proof framing MUST satisfy Placement and bundle states and Canonical encodings.
  2. Every action signature MUST verify under its corresponding rk, and bindingSigTachyon MUST verify under the derived binding key, over the containing transaction's sighash.
  3. A bundle with no actions MUST have zero valueBalanceTachyon and zero vFeeTachyon; its proof MUST establish a zero maximum fee. It still publishes a fee change note commitment and requires proof coverage.
  4. The bundle's own vTachygrams MUST contain no repeated member.

Signature checks apply independently to each covered bundle. Valid signatures and encodings do not establish proof coverage; the block rules below also apply.

#Block validity

A validator MUST enforce the following across all Tachyon bundles in a block:

  1. Unique coverage. Every txid listed by a proof stamp MUST identify a transaction in the same block with a stripped Tachyon bundle. Every stripped bundle MUST be listed by exactly one proof stamp. This applies even when it has no actions. The carrying transaction is covered implicitly and MUST NOT list itself. References to proof-stamped transactions are invalid.
  2. Tachygram uniqueness. Scan vTachygrams from every present bundle, including stripped bundles. All members MUST be distinct across the block. Cross-block duplicate checks use the shielded protocol's epoch window.
  3. Coverage reconstruction. For each proof stamp, collect its own bundle and all bundles named by its adjunct list. Action descriptors MUST be distinct. The 40-byte fee entries cvFeeMaxTachyon || LE64(vFeeTachyon) MUST also be distinct. Associate each action with its own bundle's corresponding pair, and each fee descriptor with that bundle's final tachygram. The resulting union MUST contain at most 8191 tachygrams.
  4. Tachygram commitment. Reconstruct the multiset commitment to this union and reject unless it equals the proof stamp's cTachygrams.
  5. Proof verification. Reconstruct the action/fee multiset commitment from the Action digests and Fee digests. The Ragu proof MUST verify against the reconstructed data and anchorTachyon, under the shielded protocol's proof verification rules.

A validator can enforce rule 1 by indexing all stripped bundles by txid, removing each as a proof stamp claims it, rejecting absent or already-claimed entries, and rejecting any entries left unclaimed. Checking only the lists would miss unlisted, unproved bundles. These checks belong to the node; the reference library's bundle verifier receives already-resolved adjuncts.16

After verification, the shielded protocol's accumulator rules advance pool state once per proof stamp using its covered union. Stripped bundles do not cause additional absorption of the same tachygrams.

#Rationale

This section is explanatory and introduces no additional protocol requirements.

#Transaction-local tachygrams

The action digest binds each pair to its action. Keeping those pairs on their originating transactions preserves that association after aggregation and permits retrieval by tachygram. The fee digest similarly binds the fee change commitment to its bundle.7

#Aggregate references use txid

The txid list identifies the transactions whose data are needed to verify the aggregate. A txid is stable across stripping; the proof binds the actual pairs, fees, and fee changes supplied for those transactions. A different authorization form with incompatible data fails verification. Stripped bundles need no reference rewritten when the aggregate is lifted, merged, or rerandomized.

#Pair order and list order

The sum/product action digest treats each tachygram pair as unordered. The wire format accepts either order and the authorization digest records the received bytes. The reference prover emits smaller-first pairs to avoid signaling their roles through order. Adjunct txids are strictly sorted to give their set one wire ordering.

#Covered descriptors are distinct

The multiset commitment preserves repeated roots; it does not prohibit them. Explicit checks reject repeated action and fee descriptors before proof verification. Parsers preserve action multiplicity and order.

#Zero-action bundles still need coverage

A zero-action bundle retains its maximum-fee commitment, binding signature, zero applied fee, and fee change tachygram. Its fee proof and block coverage remain necessary even though it contributes no ordinary action descriptors.

#Deterministic multiset commitments

The committed multisets are public, and deterministic commitments let validators reconstruct them from the covered bundles.

#Security Implications

This section is explanatory and introduces no additional protocol requirements.

Signatures cover effecting data. Stripping preserves that data and the signatures, while changing auth_digest and wtxid. A valid binding signature enforces the value balance, including the maximum-fee commitment; the proof establishes the applied fee and the resulting fee change note.

The adjunct list is included in auth_digest, but is not signed or itself bound by the proof. Nodes resolve it against the block, verify the actual covered bundles, and enforce exactly-once coverage. Omitting the check for unlisted stripped bundles would permit actions with no proof.16

Tachygrams remain authorization data, but their association with actions is enforced by the proof's action digests. Preserving the overall tachygram union while relocating members between actions does not preserve those digests.

Parse validity and signature validity do not establish spend validity. The Block validity rules and the shielded protocol's Consensus rules also apply.

#Privacy Implications

This section is explanatory and introduces no additional protocol requirements.

𝖼𝗏 hides the action's value. The action count, value balance, applied fee, and each bundle's tachygrams are public. The wire order reveals which two tachygrams belong to each action and identifies the fee change commitment. An aggregate's txid list reveals its covered transactions.

An action's pair does not label its members or distinguish a spend from an output in a single version. Comparing versions re-proved at different epochs can distinguish spends, whose pairs change, from outputs, whose pairs remain the same. See the shielded protocol's Privacy and Security Implications.

Key unlinkability and note privacy depend on the shielded protocol. Payload confidentiality and note discovery depend on the payment protocol.
