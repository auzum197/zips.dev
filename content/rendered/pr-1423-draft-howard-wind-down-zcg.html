#Terminology

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "SHOULD", and "MAY" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 1 when, and only when, they appear in all capitals.

"Zcash Community Grants" ("ZCG"), "ZCG Committee", and "Financial Privacy Foundation" ("FPF") have the meanings given in ZIP 1015. 2

"Coinholder-Controlled Fund" ("CCF") has the meaning given in ZIP 1016. 3

"Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund Lockbox" ("the Lockbox") means the pool specified in the draft by Sean Bowe, Zooko Wilcox, and Jason McGee. 4

"Lockbox administrator" means the entity designated by a future ZIP to administer Lockbox withdrawals and receive residual ZCG funds for future Zcash development and legal defense.

"Wind-down Date" means the UTC calendar day of Mainnet activation of the network upgrade that deploys this ZIP.

"ZCG funds" means assets held or administered by FPF for the ZCG program, including its grant and Discretionary Budget balances, assets subsequently received for that program, grant refunds, and proceeds attributable to those assets. This includes any remaining assets transferred from ZCG's previous administrator. It excludes the CCF and assets administered by FPF for other programs.

"Existing commitments" means grants approved by the ZCG Committee before activation of the designated upgrade on the Wind-down Date, including their approved but unpaid milestones, and legally binding ZCG administrative obligations entered into before that activation. An application, request for proposals, or expression of intent that has not resulted in an approved grant or binding obligation is not a commitment.

"Residual funds" means ZCG funds remaining after provision for existing commitments and the wind-down expenses permitted by this ZIP.

#Abstract

This ZIP proposes an orderly wind-down of Zcash Community Grants. At activation of the designated Mainnet network upgrade, ZCG would stop making new grants and begin winding down its operations. If ZCG's 8% funding stream is still active, the upgrade would end it and redirect the same share of the block subsidy to the Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund Lockbox for the rest of the stream's scheduled term. If the stream has already expired and the Lockbox has yet to be deployed, activation would create it with a zero opening balance.

FPF would complete existing commitments under their terms and retain full reserves for outstanding obligations and permitted wind-down expenses. FPF would hold the surplus until a future ZIP designates the Lockbox administrator and that entity establishes a receiving address. Once legally authorized, FPF would transfer surplus to that address as it becomes available. If a separately authorized NSM donation is completed before Mainnet activation, the wind-down would apply to the remaining treasury and subsequent receipts.

ZIPs 1015 and 1016 currently restrict the purposes for which FPF may use ZCG funds. This ZIP specifies amendments that expressly authorize completing existing commitments, paying limited closure costs, and transferring the remainder to the Lockbox administrator. FPF would obtain the legal instruments and approvals needed to carry out these changes.

The wind-down would begin at Mainnet activation even if the administrator's receiving address is still pending. Public reporting would account for the treasury while protecting confidential grantee and financial information.

#Motivation

ZCG has carried the torch through the evolution of Zcash's development reward program. From the Major Grants structure established by ZIP 1014 to the community grants program administered by FPF under ZIP 1015, it has provided continuity for independent teams and a way to direct protocol-funded resources toward work that benefits the Zcash ecosystem. 5 2 That contribution provides the foundation for the next stage of the funding model.

Coinholder-directed funding has been part of that model since ZIP 1016, "Community and Coinholder Funding Model", was created on 2025-02-19. 3 The voting technology developed to support it has since matured into an operational system. The Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program (CDRGP) completed its Q3 2026 round on September 29, 2026, with approximately 2.18 million ZEC of voting power participating on individual proposals. 6 7 The separate NU7 scope vote closed on September 14, 2026, with approximately 2.40 million ZEC participating on its issuance-schedule question. 8 Circulating supply was approximately 16.96 million ZEC on September 29 and 16.93 million ZEC on September 14. Both processes therefore exceeded two million ZEC of participation, or over 12% of the circulating supply in each case. 9

The CDRGP has demonstrated that coinholder-directed grants are operational. Its Q3 2026 results approve funding for core protocol work by ZODL and Valar Group, Zec.rocks wallet infrastructure, Ironwood external audits and formal verification, and critical vulnerability bounties for Orchard and Zebra. 6 Earlier rounds have also progressed from approval to completed disbursements, including payments for Unstoppable Wallet, BitcoinVN, and ZcashCommunity website hosting. 10 These are concrete examples of a functioning funding channel for core development, infrastructure, and security.

ZIP 1016 provides for open proposal submission and permissionless participation by eligible coinholders in grant approval, rather than requiring an award from the ZCG Committee. 3 The program retains published eligibility conditions, legal vetoes, and administration and disbursement by FPF and the Key-Holder Organizations. Its operation demonstrates that a separate ZCG grant-selection committee is no longer needed to provide that funding channel.

This proposal therefore treats ZCG's off-chain committee and legal arrangements as a transitional bridge that has served its purpose. The development-funding system now has an operational coinholder-directed approval process, supported by on-chain voting infrastructure. 11 Continuing a parallel ZCG grant-making body is no longer necessary in the proposed model. Winding it down recognizes its contribution and completes that transition, while preserving the legal administration still needed to execute grants.

This ZIP builds on Maxime Desalle's forum proposal, "End ZCG and send its funds to the NSM", for closing the program and honoring approved grants. 12 It uses the Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund Lockbox proposed by Bowe, Wilcox, and McGee for the remainder of the 8% allocation. The residual treasury would go to the entity designated to administer withdrawals from that Lockbox. 4

The policy proposed here is to honor work already approved, stop creating new commitments, and reserve the unused balance for future Zcash development and legal defense. Completing existing commitments gives grantees a defined transition and avoids treating program closure as a reason to abandon approved work. The Lockbox administrator would manage the residual funds for the same purposes under the terms of its designation.

The present fund restrictions need explicit attention. ZIP 1015 requires FPF to use the ZCG allocation exclusively for grants and related administration, and to recognize it contractually as a Restricted Fund donation. ZIP 1016 carries those obligations forward. 13 14 Express amendments are needed to authorize FPF to transfer the residual treasury to the Lockbox administrator.

FPF is an independent Cayman Islands foundation company. 15 Its actual authority also depends on applicable law, its constitutional documents, and the agreements governing the assets. Foundation companies have their own memorandum and articles of association. 16 This proposal provides a community mandate and a process for putting the necessary legal authority in place; it does not assume those documents have already been reviewed or amended.

#Privacy Implications

Redirecting the subsidy to the Lockbox creates no payment output. Publishing the wind-down accounts and evidence of treasury transfers may associate transactions with FPF or the ZCG treasury. Reports can establish amounts and completion without publishing grantees' private identities, payment addresses, KYC records, or privileged legal advice.

#Requirements

  • Stop new ZCG grant-making at a clearly defined cutoff.
  • If the 8% ZCG stream is still active, end it at activation and direct the same share to the Lockbox for the remainder of its scheduled term.
  • Establish the Lockbox at activation even if the 8% stream has expired.
  • Complete existing commitments under their approved terms, including normal milestone verification and remedies for non-performance.
  • Provide FPF with an explicit mandate, within the ZIP framework and the applicable legal instruments, to close ZCG and transfer residual funds to the Lockbox administrator.
  • Limit and disclose the costs of administering the wind-down.
  • Account for the full ZCG treasury, including later receipts and recoveries, without diverting funds belonging to other programs.
  • Safeguard commitments and residual funds until their disposition is complete.
  • End the committee's operational role when its remaining duties are complete, while preserving necessary accounting, custody, and reporting.
  • Preserve the CCF stream and governance.

#Specification

#Activation

The wind-down and the amendments to ZIPs 1015 and 1016 MUST take effect at Mainnet activation of the designated network upgrade, regardless of the stream's status or the availability of the Lockbox administrator's receiving address.

The deployment ZIP MUST identify this ZIP and specify the upgrade's activation height for each network under ZIP 200. 17 Existing rules governing ZCG funds continue to apply until Mainnet activation.

At Mainnet activation, ZCG MUST stop accepting applications and approving new grants. Pending applications that were not approved before activation MUST be closed without an award.

#The Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund Lockbox

The Lockbox consensus rules and their activation conditions are specified by the referenced draft. 18 4 The designated upgrade MUST deploy those rules where they have yet to take effect, subject to the amendment below. Where they already took effect, the upgrade MUST carry forward the existing Lockbox and any remaining replacement stream.

Amendment for activation after stream expiry. Where the ZCG stream has already expired at activation and the Lockbox does not yet exist, the Lockbox recipient and balance rules MUST still take effect at activation. The balance MUST be initialized to zero before applying the activation block. The referenced draft's expiry condition continues to govern the funding streams.

#Administration during the wind-down

From Mainnet activation, FPF and the ZCG Committee MUST administer ZCG funds solely to complete existing commitments, meet the permitted wind-down expenses, and safeguard and transfer residual funds as specified below.

FPF MUST notify affected applicants and existing grantees of the wind-down and publish the designated upgrade, its Mainnet activation height, the Wind-down Date when known, and a contact for outstanding commitments.

This ZIP does not alter ownership of funds already paid to grantees. Any ZCG funds received after activation MUST be included in the wind-down accounts and disposed of under this ZIP.

The process MAY be rehearsed on Testnet using test funds. Testnet activation or rehearsal MUST NOT trigger the Mainnet wind-down or authorize disposal of Mainnet ZCG funds. Dates and reporting deadlines in this ZIP MUST be interpreted using UTC.

#Inventory, reserves, and wind-down plan

Within 30 days after the Wind-down Date, FPF, with the ZCG Committee, MUST publish an initial wind-down plan containing:

  • ZCG balances by asset and currency, reconciled to the accounting records, with any separately authorized pre-activation NSM donation shown separately;
  • each existing commitment, its remaining amount and currency, outstanding conditions, and expected completion or lawful termination date, subject to the reporting protections below;
  • reserves sufficient to meet those commitments and permitted wind-down expenses, including a documented allowance for contingent liabilities and currency risk;
  • an itemized wind-down budget, with a spending cap, responsible parties, and an expected timetable for completing the administrative work; and
  • the prerequisites for transferring residual funds, identifying which have been satisfied and which remain outstanding.

The plan MUST distinguish an approved grant from an unconditional payment liability. Its reserve MUST provide for unpaid approved milestones without waiving their conditions. FPF MUST reserve assets needed to satisfy creditors, existing commitments, and other legal obligations before transferring surplus. If assets are insufficient to meet these obligations, FPF MUST report the shortfall and address it under the applicable agreements and law before determining that any residual funds are available.

FPF MUST keep ZCG funds separately accounted for and MUST NOT use them for the CCF, other FPF programs, or general FPF operations unrelated to the wind-down.

#Completion of existing commitments

FPF and the ZCG Committee MUST continue to administer existing commitments under their approved terms, including milestone review, reporting, and any applicable suspension, termination, or refund provisions. This ZIP does not make conditional milestones payable in advance or excuse non-performance.

An existing commitment MAY be amended to enable completion or lawful closure, but an amendment MUST NOT increase its approved funding amount or add a new project or new scope of work. Any change requiring a grantee's consent MUST obtain that consent. An unexercised option for additional funding or a discretionary renewal MUST NOT be exercised.

For an indefinite or automatically renewing commitment, FPF MUST use the earliest lawful non-renewal or termination opportunity consistent with its existing obligations. The plan MUST identify the resulting final service and payment dates. Disputed or contingent liabilities MUST remain reserved until resolved or lawfully discharged.

FPF MUST account for amounts released by completion, cancellation, refunds, or a reduction in a required reserve when recalculating residual funds.

#Wind-down expenses and committee closure

ZCG funds MAY cover reasonable, documented expenses necessary to complete the wind-down, including grant administration, legal and accounting work, asset conversion, custody, transaction fees, final reporting, and required record retention. FPF MUST limit charges to work attributable to ZCG's wind-down and MUST NOT charge unrelated programs' costs to ZCG.

The ZCG Committee MUST approve the initial wind-down budget and any increase in its spending cap, subject to FPF's existing legal veto. Existing conflict of interest and compensation rules MUST continue to apply. Committee and FPF compensation MUST reflect the reduced duties and actual work required.

Until its remaining decisions are complete, the committee MUST retain the membership and decision-making arrangements required by ZIP 1015. A vacancy or expiring term MUST be addressed under the existing selection process if needed to perform those duties. The wind-down does not authorize FPF to take over discretionary grant decisions.

Once existing commitments have been completed or lawfully discharged and the committee has approved the final administrative budget, the committee MUST conclude its operational role. The final budget MUST provide for any remaining custody, transfers, reporting, and legal record retention. FPF MUST retain responsibility for those duties. After committee closure, any increase to the cap for spending from ZCG funds MUST be authorized through an amendment to this ZIP. FPF MUST disclose any projected shortfall in its quarterly report.

#Legal authority and implementation

The 50,000 ZEC NSM donation described in the Bowe, Wilcox, and McGee draft may be completed before Mainnet activation if separately approved and lawful under the restrictions applicable at the time. 19 This ZIP governs the ZCG funds remaining at Mainnet activation and any later receipts or recoveries.

Before transferring residual funds, FPF MUST obtain advice from qualified Cayman Islands counsel concerning its authority to execute this wind-down, including the restricted-fund terms, its constitutional documents, existing contracts, and any required consents or approvals.

FPF MUST arrange for the commitments required by ZIPs 1015 and 1016 to be amended, in suitable legal language, to authorize the uses specified here. It MUST obtain any additional consent, corporate approval, or court order that is necessary before taking an action that requires it. A ZIP amendment MUST NOT be treated as automatically amending an agreement, extinguishing a third party's rights, or overriding applicable law.

FPF MUST publish confirmation of the authority and approvals on which it relies, without disclosing privileged advice or confidential information. If a required authorization cannot be obtained, FPF MUST report the obstacle, safeguard the affected funds, and seek a further community decision. Such an obstacle MUST NOT authorize new grants or an alternative recipient.

#Transfer of residual funds to the Lockbox administrator

FPF MUST hold residual funds in segregated custody pending transfer and report the balance and custody costs quarterly. Before transferring funds, FPF MUST obtain the legal authority required above and verify a Mainnet receiving address established and controlled by the entity designated in an accepted ZIP.

FPF MUST transfer residual funds to that address in ZEC, retaining sufficient reserves for all outstanding obligations and permitted wind-down expenses. FPF MUST convert residual assets other than ZEC on commercially reasonable terms, with conversion costs included in the approved budget and disclosed in the accounts. The designated entity MUST account for the received funds separately from the Lockbox's on-chain balance and administer them for future Zcash development and legal defense under the terms of the accepted ZIP and applicable legal instruments.

FPF MUST publish a transfer schedule in the wind-down plan and update it as reserves are released. Each residual amount MUST be transferred within 90 days after it becomes available without impairing required reserves and the designation, address verification, and legal prerequisites above are met. FPF MAY combine amounts within that period to reduce transaction costs.

For each transfer, FPF MUST verify confirmation on the canonical chain and publish its amount, transaction identifier, confirmation block, and evidence of receipt by the designated entity. The same rules apply to later receipts and recoveries.

#Transparency and final accounting

The existing transparency, security, and responsible disclosure obligations in ZIPs 1015 and 1016 MUST continue during the wind-down. Reports MUST protect confidential grantee information, private payment details, and key material; redactions MUST NOT prevent reconciliation of the aggregate financial amounts.

FPF MUST publish quarterly progress reports reconciling opening balances, receipts, commitment payments, expenses, conversion proceeds, transfers to the Lockbox administrator, reserves, and closing balances. FPF MUST explain any missed timetable or change in reserve or budget.

After existing commitments and wind-down expenses have been settled and all residual funds then held have been transferred, FPF MUST publish a final reconciliation and closure report. It MUST identify the custodian and retention arrangements for legally required records and provide a contact for later receipts, recoveries, or claims. Public reports and grant records SHOULD remain available as a historical archive. Any later receipt or recovery MUST be accounted for and transferred under this ZIP after provision for lawful related expenses. Custody and quarterly reporting obligations MUST continue for any such funds pending transfer.

#Changes to ZIP 1015

In the Specification, under "Zcash Community Grants (ZCG)", insert a new subsection "Wind-down of Zcash Community Grants" immediately before "Furthering Decentralization", with the following text:

At Mainnet activation on the Wind-down Date defined in the ZIP "Winding Down Zcash Community Grants", FPF and the ZCG Committee MUST begin winding down the ZCG program in accordance with that ZIP. For this limited purpose, its provisions take precedence over conflicting provisions of this ZIP, including the exclusive grant-use restriction, committee and election requirements, Discretionary Budget provisions, and obligations to explore an independent ZCG entity.

The permitted purposes of the ZCG Restricted Fund donation expressly include completing existing commitments, paying the reasonable and limited wind-down expenses authorized by that ZIP, and transferring all residual funds to the entity designated to administer withdrawals from the Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund Lockbox. FPF MUST arrange the corresponding amendments to the restricted-fund agreements and obtain the legal authority required by that ZIP before executing an action that requires it. These purposes do not authorize new grants, unrelated FPF expenditure, or diversion to another fund.

A transfer mandated by that ZIP is an authorized disposition of the Restricted Fund. Compliance with that ZIP satisfies the prescribed-use requirement for the purposes of Enforcement. Security, separate accounting, and transparency obligations continue until their completion under that ZIP, with required record retention continuing thereafter.

Add a reference to this ZIP in that subsection and in the References section, using its assigned identifier when available. The existing funding-stream tables and historical allocations are unchanged by this amendment.

In "Administrative obligations and constraints", append to the first paragraph:

The ZCG wind-down amendments to ZIP 1015 and the ZIP "Winding Down Zcash Community Grants" apply only to ZCG funds. #Rationale

#Completing approved work

Staged transfers let FPF move available surplus while keeping a documented reserve for approved work, potential liabilities, and wind-down expenses. Existing projects can finish under their milestone conditions. Closing applications and discretionary renewals provides a definite end to grant-making. Contractual termination provisions address ongoing arrangements without assuming that all grants end on the same date.

#Funding future development and legal defense

The Lockbox holds the subsidy allocation for future development and legal defense. The entity designated to administer its withdrawals would manage the residual ZCG treasury separately for those same purposes. 4

#Expressly amending the fund restrictions

Express permission in both ZIPs and the corresponding legal instruments gives FPF a mandate to transfer the restricted funds to the designated entity. The CCF exception confines the wind-down to ZCG funds when ZIP 1016 incorporates ZIP 1015's rules.

#Coordinating the wind-down with a network upgrade

Mainnet activation ends new grant-making and, if the ZCG stream is still active, redirects its remaining subsidy allocation to the Lockbox.

FPF may need to hold the residual treasury after the committee concludes its operational role while a future ZIP designates the Lockbox administrator and the entity establishes a receiving address. The wind-down budget accounts for that custody.

#Community consideration

This is an initial proposal for discussion; it does not claim community consensus. Review should consider the effect on teams that depend on future ZCG grants, the availability of other funding channels, the proposed cutoff, and the costs and risks of retaining funds pending transfer.

#Deployment

This ZIP is not included in NU7 as currently specified by ZIP 259. 21 The target upgrade is after NU7. Its deployment ZIP will specify the activation height for each network under ZIP 200. 17 The upgrade will establish the Lockbox where needed and redirect any remaining ZCG stream as specified above. Mainnet activation starts the wind-down even if the Lockbox administrator has yet to be designated.

#Reference implementation

None yet.
